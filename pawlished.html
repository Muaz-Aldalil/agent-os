    Why Dogs Walk In Wagging

    Gentle handling built into the room architecture.

    We designed our Mercer Row conservatory with veterinary behaviorists so anxious, senior, and first-time dogs never experience sensory overload.

    pH 7.0 Botanical Apothecary

    Human shampoos strip canine lipid barriers. We formulate small-batch washes balanced to canine pH 7.0 using colloidal oatmeal, organic aloe, and cold-pressed Oregon cedarwood.

    Zero Cage Dryers, Ever

    Heated box dryers are banned from our building. Every dog is hand-dried using warm Belgian waffle towels and insulated acoustic-diffuser wands capped at 52 dB.

    Fear-Free Certified Stylists

    Our stylists hold Fear-Free Pets certification and work on one dog at a time. If your dog needs a 10-minute water break or chin rub mid-trim, the clock pauses for them.

    Open-Glass Conservatory

    No hidden back rooms. Every grooming table faces our sunlit conservatory garden so you can watch from the espresso bench or receive an SMS the moment your dog is ready.
